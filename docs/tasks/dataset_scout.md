# Dataset scout: choose the group-work dataset

**Task.** Find ONE public dataset of students working in small groups, for the talk + vision test.
Laptop only. Do not download videos (too big): only small files (transcripts, labels).

## Must have
1. Students working together in small groups (ideally university).
2. **Transcripts with who spoke (speaker ID) and when (start, end time).**
3. **Labels about collaboration or participation** (per utterance, per student or per group).
4. Licence allows academic use; downloadable this week.

## Nice to have
5. Video of the group (lets us run our vision model).
6. Automatic transcripts too (e.g. Whisper / Google).
7. Transcripts and labels downloadable separately from the video.

## Candidates (look for others too)
- **Weights Task Dataset (WTD):** https://zenodo.org/records/10252341 · paper: https://openhumanitiesdata.metajnl.com/articles/10.5334/johd.168
  Already checked: `Group_XX_Oracle.csv` has `Utterance, Start, End, Group, Participant, Transcript`;
  `All_Groups_CPS.csv` has 19 collaboration codes per utterance (same rows); participant 4 = experimenter.
  Video: 3 mp4 per group, not yet opened. Ask Mambo for the small CSV files.
- **AMI Meeting Corpus:** https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/ (adult meetings, not students)
- **Oulu collaborative-learning dataset:** https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.05419 (high-school groups; availability unclear)

## Send to the group (½ page)
Per candidate: criteria 1–7 (yes / no / unknown), size, licence, link, risks. Then your recommendation.
